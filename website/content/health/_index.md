# Portal Health & Reachability

Hourly reachability sampling of Flock Safety transparency portals.

Reachability is not the same as availability of data. A portal that returns HTTP 200
may still have removed its sharing partners, changed its hotlist subscriptions, or
altered its prohibited-use policy — those changes are tracked separately in the
[agency directory]({{% relref "/agencies" %}}) and the [NCIC compliance matrix]({{% relref "/ncic" %}}).

This dashboard answers a narrower question: can we still reach the portal at all, or has
Cloudflare started blocking us?
