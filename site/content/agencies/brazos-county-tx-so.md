### Policy Overview
* **Prohibited Uses**: Flock technology may not be used by the Brazos County Sheriff’s Office for purposes outside of legitimate law-enforcement and public-safety needs. Prohibited uses include:

-Immigration enforcement
-Issuing traffic citations. The Flock cameras utilized by the Brazos County Sheriff’s Office do not have the capability to issue citations for traffic violations.
-Harassment, intimidation, or retaliation
-Targeting or conducting searches based solely on a protected characteristic, including race, sex, religion, or other protected status
-Personal use or access for reasons unrelated to official law-enforcement duties
* **Acceptable Use**: Flock data is used by the Brazos County Sheriff’s Office for legitimate law-enforcement purposes, including criminal investigations, locating missing individuals, and other public-safety needs. Access to and use of the system is governed by agency policies and operating procedures designed to ensure the technology is used appropriately and responsibly.
* **Access Policy**: Access to the Flock system is limited to authorized personnel and requires a valid law-enforcement purpose. All searches are logged to support accountability and appropriate use of the system. Search logs are retained indefinitely.
