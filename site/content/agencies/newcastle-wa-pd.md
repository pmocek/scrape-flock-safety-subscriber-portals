### Policy Overview
* **Prohibited Uses**: Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.
* **Acceptable Use**: Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Newcastle PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.
* **Access Policy**: All system access requires a valid reason and is only granted to authorized law enforcement personnel. While license plates, etc. are only retained for the agency's retention period, individual user activity (for internal auditing by Newcastle PD) is stored indefinitely.
