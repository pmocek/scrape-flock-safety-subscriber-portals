### Policy Overview
* **Prohibited Uses**: Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harrassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.
* **Acceptable Use**: Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Yakima WA PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.
* **Access Policy**: All system access requires an individual to be a member of the police department and a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.
