### Policy Overview
* **Prohibited Uses**: ALPR technology and data shall not be used for the sole purpose of immigration enforcement.
* **Acceptable Use**: Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Mill Creek WA PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.
* **Access Policy**: Access to the Mill Creek Flock Safety network is restricted to Snohomish County LE agencies who have completed and signed a Data Sharing Agreement. All inquiries must have valid LE nexus and state the reason for search. This data is audited and retained per WA State law.
