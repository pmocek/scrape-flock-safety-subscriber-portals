Overview

The Olympia Police Department is committed to transparency and as such uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Olympia officers may conduct searches of the Flock Safety database to solve crimes after they have occurred. Additionally, the Olympia Police Department utilizes Flock Safety real time alerting of vehicles associated with Amber and Silver Alerts, stolen vehicles, and vehicles involved in other criminal acts. In an effort to ensure proper usage of Flock Safety technology, the Olympia Police Department implemented the Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR) Policy linked below. The Olympia Police Department publishes the department's ALPR statistics on their transparency website monthly.

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Olympia WA PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
