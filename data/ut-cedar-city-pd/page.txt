Overview

UT - Cedar City PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. UT - Cedar City PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, UT - Cedar City PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by UT - Cedar City PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits (i.e., stolen vehicles, missing persons, etc.) are required to be human verified prior to action.
