Overview

The St. Peters Police Department uses Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR) technology as one of many tools available to officers and investigators to help investigate and solve crimes. ALPR cameras capture license plate and vehicle information from vehicles traveling in areas visible to the public. This technology can help officers locate stolen vehicles, identify vehicles associated with criminal activity or investigations, and assist in locating missing or endangered persons.
