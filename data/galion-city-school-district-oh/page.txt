Overview

Galion City School District, in partnership with the Galion Police Department (GPD), utilizes Flock Safety technology to enhance safety in and around school property while maintaining appropriate privacy safeguards.

Flock Safety license plate reader technology captures objective vehicle information that may be searched retroactively by law enforcement to assist with criminal investigations. Real-time alerts for specifically identified or hot-listed vehicles may also assist officers in locating wanted individuals, responding to crimes in progress, and supporting patrol operations.

The system does not identify vehicle owners or occupants, and license plates captured by the system are not automatically queried for registered owner information.
Live-feed cameras are also utilized to provide recorded video of intersections surrounding the school, including areas where students regularly use crosswalks.

As part of our commitment to transparency and responsible use of this technology, information regarding system usage and applicable safeguards is made available to the public through this portal.

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Galion City School District (OH) and is never sold to 3rd parties. Law enforcement restricted data, such as local, national, and statewide hotlists, are only accessible to certified law enforcement personnel.

Prohibited Uses

Civil immigration enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), initiating enforcement action for minor traffic violations, personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
