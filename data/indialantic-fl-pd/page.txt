Overview

The Indialantic Police Department's use of Flock ALPR technology was suspended on September 1, 2026 until further notice.

The following directive from Chief Michael Connor was issued to all Indialantic Police Department personnel on September 1, 2026 concerning the use of the Flock Safety Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR) program utilized by the Indialantic Police Department:

Effective immediately, all Indialantic Police Department personnel are directed to discontinue use of the Flock Safety Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR) system until further notice. Personnel shall not access, search, query, or otherwise use the Flock system during this suspension unless specifically authorized by the Chief of Police. All user accounts have been suspended until further notice.

This decision was not made lightly. The Flock ALPR system is a useful law-enforcement tool and an important force multiplier, particularly for an agency of our size. It has assisted our officers and investigators in investigating multiple major crimes within the Indialantic community.

I have spent several days carefully weighing the advantages and disadvantages of suspending the program, including its proven value to law enforcement and the responsibilities associated with its use. After considering these factors, I have determined that an immediate suspension is the appropriate course of action at this time.
The suspension will remain in effect until personnel are formally notified otherwise. All officers and staff are expected to comply with this directive.

If you have any questions, please feel free to contact me directly.
Michael A. Connor
Chief of Police
