Overview

Golden CO PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Golden CO PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Golden CO PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

Community Impact
Stolen Property Recovery

Featured Success Stories

In broad daylight, a box truck was broken into at a local hotel. A rifle and other items were stolen from the box truck. A suspect vehicle was identified leaving the area on a local FLOCK camera. That afternoon officers sent out a bolo with the vehicle description. A local law enforcement agency utilized FLOCK to locate the vehicle in their jurisdiction. With cooperation from the Golden Special Operations Unit, surveillance was conducted to confirm that the vehicle was 100% the vehicle used in the break-in. The vehicle was impounded and the Golden Investigations Unit executed a search warrant to obtain evidence of the crime. Without FLOCK, this crime would remain open and unsolved.

What's Detected

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Golden CO PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

This sample of prohibited uses includes but is not limited to: Immigration enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), personal use.

Access

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
