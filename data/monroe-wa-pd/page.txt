Overview

The Monroe PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising individual privacy. The Monroe PD uses retroactive searches to help generate case leads and solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, the Monroe PD utilizes real-time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals who travel through our city to commit other crimes.

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Monroe WA PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e., race, sex, religion), or personal use.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits (i.e., stolen vehicles, missing persons, etc.) are required to be human verified prior to action.
