OVERVIEW

The Newcastle Police Department is using Flock Automatic License Plate Readers (ALPRs) to help detect and reduce crime in our community. Flock ALPRs are stationary ALPR cameras located within Municipal - Right of Ways and are self-sufficient. They are powered by solar panels and send image information through government-standard encrypted cell signals. The ALPRs capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Newcastle Police Department utilizes retroactive searches to solve crimes after they have occurred. Additionally, Newcastle Police Department utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals and missing endangered children and adults.

POLICIES

What's Detected

Rear License Plates, Rear of Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Newcastle PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is only granted to authorized law enforcement personnel. While license plates, etc. are only retained for the agency's retention period, individual user activity (for internal auditing by Newcastle PD) is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
