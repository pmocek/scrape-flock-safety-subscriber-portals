OVERVIEW

Lakewood WA PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Lakewood WA PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Lakewood WA PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below usage statistics available to the public.

POLICIES

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race, Personal Information

Acceptable Use

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Lakewood WA PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harrassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access

All system access requires a valid reason and the audit trail is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.

Recent Success Story

In the summer of 2024, Lakewood Police responded to a call for service which resulted in a homicide investigation. During the investigation, LPD personnel learned that a specific vehicle was used by the suspects to flee the scene from witnesses on scene. Unfortunately, no plate information was available, and the neighboring businesses surveillance clarity was limited to description of its make and model. However, when LPD personnel searched the Flock Safety Camera system in our city, a matching make and model was quickly located leaving the scene when the homicide occurred. This provided LPD with a license plate number.

By combining this information with other information obtained through warrants, LPD personnel were able to confirm the exact vehicle involved in the homicide. By identifying the vehicle early in the investigation, other surveillance videos were able to be located and helped to identify the suspects involved. This success would not have been possible without Flock. The suspects in the homicide were later arrested and booked into jail.
