Overview

The Upper Arlington Police Division uses Flock Safety technology to help investigate crimes and enhance public safety while protecting individual privacy. The system enables officers to conduct retroactive searches of vehicle data to assist in solving crimes after they occur and provides real-time alerts when vehicles associated with wanted persons or criminal activity are detected.

To promote transparency and accountability, the Upper Arlington Police Division has made its Flock Safety policies, usage statistics and other program information available to the public.

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Upper Arlington OH PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.
