Overview

The Kent Police Department uses ALPR cameras to locate missing or endangered persons (such as those associated with AMBER or Silver Alerts), to locate wanted suspects, to locate stolen vehicles and stolen license plates, and to identify vehicles involved in felony or gross misdemeanor crimes.

The Kent Police Department has registered its ALPR systems with the Washington State Office of the Attorney General as required by Washington State law and is committed to full compliance with all applicable laws and policy governing responsible ALPR use.

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by the Kent Police Department and is never sold to third parties.

Prohibited Uses

Civil immigration enforcement; automated traffic infraction ticketing; personal use; non-law enforcement purposes; harassment or intimidation; discrimination; usage based solely on a protected class (such as race, sex, or religion).

Access Policy

The system may only be accessed by Kent Police officers who have been trained in its use, for official and legitimate law enforcement purposes, and for a valid reason associated with an official case report or investigation.

Hotlist Policy

All "hotlist" hits are required to be verified by an officer prior to any law enforcement action being taken.
