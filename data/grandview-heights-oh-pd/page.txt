Overview

Grandview Heights OH PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Grandview Heights OH PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Grandview Heights OH PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

Recent Success Stories

While investigating the attempted abduction of an adult female, FLOCK was used to assist in identifying the suspect vehicle and an independent witness.
Initial reports indicated the suspect attempted to drag the victim to his waiting vehicle. The victim escaped and ran to another occupied vehicle in the area, where she asked the driver for assistance. The suspect then fled the area in his vehicle. After the victim reached safety, the witness also left the area prior to police arrival.

Investigators used building surveillance cameras to help identify a potential suspect vehicle circulating the area before the crime. Investigators then used FLOCK to obtain license plate information and identify a person of interest.

FLOCK further assisted in identifying the potential witness by alerting investigators to the fact that the witness’s vehicle belonged to a rental company. Investigators contacted the rental company, which, after providing legal compliance, provided the information for the person who rented the vehicle. Investigators contacted this person in Texas. The witness fully corroborated the victim’s account, provided a statement, and picked the suspect from a photo lineup. This was instrumental in the case and helped secure a conviction for Abduction.

While conducting a homicide investigation, FLOCK was used to identify several vehicles and witnesses who passed through the crime scene. These witnesses to a violent felony were identified and provided statements that were critical to the investigation, arrest, and successful trial, leading to a conviction for Reckless Homicide.

The offender is currently incarcerated in prison.

While conducting a burglary and theft investigation, investigators obtained a 20-count indictment after identifying two suspects who traveled from California to steal multiple credit cards, cash, and personal items from businesses in Grandview Heights and the City of Columbus. FLOCK helped identify the suspect’s vehicle and assisted in obtaining probable cause for a search warrant that resulted in the recovery of evidence.

A local resident reported their vehicle was stolen overnight. Residential surveillance provided a description of a suspicious vehicle driving in the immediate area during the suspected timeframe of the theft. Detectives searched the FLOCK cameras for the same suspicious vehicle and were able to capture the license plate entering the City of Grandview Heights.

The victim's vehicle was later found abandoned at a location in the City of Columbus. While conducting the investigation, our detectives used the information provided by FLOCK and determined the victim's vehicle was found just a few houses away from the registered owner of the suspicious vehicle. Detectives conducted a "knock and talk" at the home and identified a suspect. The suspect admitted to stealing the victim's vehicle and being in possession of other stolen property. After detectives executed a search warrant at the home, they seized stolen property and linked multiple theft offenses to other jurisdictions in the Central Ohio area.

Patrol officers received a hot list alert for a stolen vehicle entering the jurisdiction. They quickly located the vehicle and, after verifying the hot list entry, conducted a felony traffic stop. Two occupants were detained and found to be in possession of methamphetamine. The vehicle was recovered within 24 hours of being reported stolen, and two suspects were arrested.
_____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

While conducting a theft of over $3,000 of landscaping equipment from a local business, surveillance video provided a description of the suspect’s vehicle; however, the license plate was not readable. FLOCK cameras were utilized using the vehicle description and known timeframe to identify the vehicle. The information helped identify three suspects. Officers charged those individuals with felony theft.
_____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

Detectives successfully used FLOCK cameras to investigate a missing/runaway juvenile who was attempting to leave the state with another juvenile from a neighboring community. Officers obtained a description of the vehicle the juveniles were believed to be traveling in. A FLOCK search supplied investigators with a vehicle image and a timeline of when it was last seen in our City. A Be On The Lookout (BOLO) was forwarded to all law enforcement. State troopers detected the vehicle, conducted a traffic stop, and returned the children to their respective families.
_____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

Patrol officers received a hot list alert for a stolen vehicle entering the jurisdiction through one of our business districts. The officers circulated the area and found the vehicle parked behind a building containing multiple closed businesses. The hot list alert was confirmed. Officers secured a perimeter around the building and began conducting surveillance. Three suspects were seen exiting the rear of one of the businesses and entering the stolen vehicle. Officers approached and detained them without further incident. Ultimately, arrests were made and one of the suspects had outstanding warrants in a neighboring jurisdiction for burglary and grand theft.

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Grandview Heights OH PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, general traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
