Overview

Coldwater MI PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Coldwater MI PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Coldwater MI PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.
